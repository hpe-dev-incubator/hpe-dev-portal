Increasingly demanding workloads, including virtualization and artificial intelligence, are prompting organizations to modernize and refresh their aging IT infrastructure. Additionally, mounting demands for improved data center efficiency and IT sustainability further intensify these challenges.

Servers form the foundation of any enterprise, ensuring the operation of critical applications and supporting valuable insights and services. However, as organizations grow, legacy server environments often fail to meet evolving requirements. Continued reliance on outdated servers can result in decreased performance, efficiency, and security, making it difficult to support contemporary workloads such as application modernization, virtualization, AI, and data analytics.

The next-generation HPE ProLiant Compute portfolio, together with HPE Compute Ops Management, HPE iLO, and comprehensive expert and digital services, offers organizations an intelligent and competitive approach to infrastructure advancement. These systems are engineered to address the performance, reliability, efficiency, and security expectations of modern workloads, enabling businesses to maintain a leading position in today’s dynamic landscape.
