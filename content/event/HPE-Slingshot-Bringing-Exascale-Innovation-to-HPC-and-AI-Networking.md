Modern HPC and AI systems depend on more than powerful processors and accelerators. Their ability to move data efficiently across thousands of compute elements is often what determines overall application and system performance. This session introduces HPE Slingshot, HPE's high-performance interconnect for large-scale HPC and AI environments. Building on decades of Cray interconnect innovation, HPE Slingshot became the first Ethernet-based interconnect to power the world's leading exascale supercomputers, combining the openness and interoperability of Ethernet with the performance, scalability, and resilience required by the most demanding computing workloads.
