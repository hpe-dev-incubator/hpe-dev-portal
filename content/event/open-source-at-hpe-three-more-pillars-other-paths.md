Join us for a cross-pillar conversation on HPE’s strategic expansion in open source. The session highlights key initiatives and communities HPE supports and contributes to, including Chapel (now part of the Linux Foundation), through the High Performance Software Foundation (HPSF) project, and SONiC as an open-source networking operating system that is driving infrastructure innovation. It also covers how HPE’s sponsorship of PyTorch to support AI and machine learning progress.
