#### Workshop details

Join us for a hands-on HPE AI Developer Day and learn how to build an agentic AI solution in a sovereign environment.

This workshop is designed for developers, AI/ML Engineers, AI team leads, data scientists, and business innovators who want to see how enterprise-grade AI solutions are built, scaled, and applied to solve real business challenges.

**What You'll Gain:**

- **See AI in Action in a sovereign environment** – Watch live demos of advanced AI use cases solving real-world problems with speed and accuracy.
- **Get Hands-On Experience** – Explore the HPE Private Cloud stack, navigate the development environment, and build practical solutions.
- **Work on Enterprise Scenarios** – Learn how AI can transform unified analytics, workflow optimization, data engineering at scale, ML pipelines, and intelligent automation.
- **Build Your Own AI Agents** – Design streamlined AI workflows, then enhance them into enterprise-ready solutions using LangFlow and HPE Private Cloud AI.
- **Walk Away with Practical Skills** – Gain applied knowledge you can use immediately in your organization.

Bring your own laptop—we'll provide full access to HPE Private Cloud AI during the day.
