Join this session to learn how HPE provides the possibility to use OpenBMC on selected HPE ProLiant servers.
After a quick recall of the Baseboard Management Controller (BMC) concept, this Meetup will present the OpenBMC project and its declination on selected HPE ProLiant servers. A demo of the public OSFCI infrastructure will be performed to demonstrate how developers can test their owned OpenBMC firmware without the need of any physical interaction with hardware.
