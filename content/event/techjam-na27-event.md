Turn expertise into impact.

HPE Tech Jam 2027 is about transforming expertise into a real-world impact. This is your chance to turn knowledge into action, connect with HPE Developer Community Managers on the showfloor in our HPE Developer Community HackShack. Meet us in Nashville and participate to our different activities like quick tour of the progran, 10 minutes' workshops, blogathon, or white board discussions. The HPE Developer community team will also deliver a breakout and a demo theater session. As always, come and meet us to learn with fun!
