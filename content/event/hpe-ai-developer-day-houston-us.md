#### Workshop details

Join this hands-on workshop to build and deploy Agentic AI solutions for healthcare using the secure, turnkey HPE Private Cloud AI (PCAI) platform. Working directly with Sickbay's clinical data analytics capabilities, participants will learn to develop autonomous agents that analyze patient data, automate clinical workflows, and optimize decision-making.

**Key Takeaways:**

- **Build Agentic AI** – Create intelligent agents capable of processing clinical information and assisting in patient care workflows.
- **HPE PCAI Hands-on** – Experience the speed and security of a tailored HPE PCAI environment co-engineered with NVIDIA.
- **Sickbay Integration** – Utilize Sickbay's clinical data integration to build predictive, actionable AI models.
- **Secure Deployment** – Learn best practices for deploying compliant, high-performance AI in healthcare settings.

Bring your own laptop—we'll provide full access to HPE Private Cloud AI during the workshop.
